# AuraKode identity

The symbol joins an angular **A** and **K** in a single, forward-moving mark. The open construction gives the name a recognizable silhouette without relying on a stock technology icon. It works in one color and at small sizes.

## Core assets

- `public/brand/aurakode-mark.svg` — deep blue mark for light backgrounds.
- `public/brand/aurakode-mark-light.svg` — two-tone mark for dark backgrounds.
- `public/brand/aurakode-lockup.svg` — horizontal lockup for presentations and social material.
- `public/brand/aurakode-lockup-ink.svg` — horizontal lockup for light backgrounds.
- `app/icon.svg` — square browser and app icon.

## Palette

| Role | Color |
| --- | --- |
| Night | `#0A1724` |
| Paper | `#F3F3EE` |
| Ice | `#F0FAFB` |
| Signal | `#73E7F5` |
| Deep blue | `#0B83AE` |

Use Signal as an accent, not as body text on a light surface. Keep the mark free of glows, bevels, and outlines. Give it at least one stroke-width of clear space on every side.

## Type and voice

Use Plus Jakarta Sans for headings and the wordmark, Inter for reading text, and JetBrains Mono only for compact annotations. The voice should be direct and specific: name the actual deliverable or decision instead of promising vague transformation.
